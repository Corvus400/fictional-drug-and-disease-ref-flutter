# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Fictional Drug And Disease Ref is a portfolio and demonstration application for
browsing fictional drug and disease data. The content in this app is sample data
only and is not medical advice.

## Data Collection

This app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app stores the following data locally on the user's device:

- Bookmarked fictional drug and disease records
- Browsing history inside the app
- Calculation history for the built-in BMI, eGFR, and CrCl tools

This locally stored data is used only to provide app features on the device. It
is not uploaded to this repository owner, App Store Connect, GitHub, or any third
party by the app.

## Network Access

The production build connects to the app backend to download fictional drug and
disease reference data. Requests are used only to provide app content. The app
does not require sign-in and does not send account credentials.

## Analytics and Advertising

This app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or
tracking for advertising purposes.

## Medical Disclaimer

All drug, disease, and clinical information in this app is fictional sample data.
The app must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, medication
decisions, or any other medical decision.
